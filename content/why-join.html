Why Join Us

The best of both worlds

Joining FIRST® Team 79 Krunch (a.k.a. The Interact Club of Krunch Robotics) offers the unique benefit of being part of both a FIRST® FRC Robotics Team and a Rotary Interact Club.

Benefits of FIRST®

Skills that last a lifetime

Cultivating an interest in STEM-related fields has a positive lifestyle impact on students. Involvement with FIRST® exposes students to an environment where they learn skills to improve their chances of pursuing a college education and securing a high-paying job. It also helps steer students clear of negative activities that may prevent them from reaching their full potential. Having qualified engineering mentors as positive role models allows students to master the skills required for success in the workplace.

Real engineering skills

Under the mentorship of local engineering professionals and returning alumni, members learn problem-solving, critical thinking, and technical skills in sub-teams of Mechanical, Electrical, and Software Engineering.

Professional skills

Skills in public speaking, communications, business writing, marketing, networking, leadership, and problem solving make students more attractive candidates for careers in STEM fields.

90%+ pursue STEM

Most Team 79 Krunch members continue on to college, with over 90% pursuing a STEM career.

Over $100 million in scholarships

Over 100 million dollars of scholarships are available to FIRST® participants.

Give back

Opportunities to earn volunteer hours and support causes you are passionate about.

A pipeline for employers

Local businesses benefit too: they can hire employees who already have the skills, experience, and education needed to succeed.

Benefits of Interact

Leadership, service, and a global network

Interact is an international organization of service, part of Rotary International, which fosters leadership and responsible citizenship and promotes international understanding and peace. One of the best things about Interact is that it connects young leaders with Rotary's global community, enabling them to build relationships, learn about other cultures, and exchange ideas with peers worldwide.

Valuable life skills

Leadership, self-development, time management, personal finances, and more.

Community service

Learn the importance of serving your community.

Cultures & understanding

Discover new cultures and international understanding.

Build your network

Make new friends and connect with leaders in your community and around the world.

Scholarships

Access scholarship opportunities through Interact and Rotary.
