Milestones

Highlights from our history

2005

Hunter's Humanitarian Award & Extreme Makeover

Krunch received the Hunter's Humanitarian Award from Lexington Homes, Inc. and the AmeriLex Foundation for designing and building a robotics-themed bedroom. ABC-TV invited us onto "Extreme Makeover: Home Edition" to transform a 12- by 14-foot bedroom for 12-year-old Charlie Dolan, whose father was blinded in a shooting, into a cyber-wonderland featuring a 5-foot ceiling-mounted robotic arm over a 10-foot L-shaped clear acrylic desk with a hidden playing field of miniature robots the students built. (Season 2, Episode 21, Parts 1 & 2.)

2012–2013

Recognized by three counties

Pinellas and Hillsborough Counties honored Krunch members for inspiring young people and promoting STEM education, establishing a special "Robotic and STEM Education" week. In 2013, Pasco County recognized our summer internship program with corporate sponsors and community partners. These recognitions from three counties reached over 2.6 million people in our community.

2015

MITRE underwater vehicle internship

Partnering with MITRE Corporation, FIRST® Robotics, Kettering University, and the Career Technical Education Foundation (CTEF), several members completed an 8-month internship designing, building, and programming an unmanned underwater vehicle capable of exploring depths up to 100 feet. After three months it performed before college professors, engineering-company heads, and government sponsors, and the team presented their work at MacDill Air Force Base to Army and Navy officials.

SOCOM

The TALOS "Iron Man" suit project

Building on that success, CTEF and MITRE joined with U.S. Special Operations Command (SOCOM) to task Krunch members, alongside five other Florida FRC teams, with researching, designing, and prototyping parts of a Tactical Assault Light Operator Suit (TALOS) to reduce human exposure in hazardous situations.
