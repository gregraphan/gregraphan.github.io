Sponsors & Support

Help us build the next generation of engineers

FIRST® Team 79 Krunch is a nonprofit, all-volunteer program with an annual budget in excess of $40,000. Corporate sponsorships, grants, in-kind gifts, and individual donations keep our students competing and serving their community. 100% of funds go directly to the team.

Why Sponsor Us

Sponsorship is a two-way street

Your support builds our program, and it also helps your organization be seen as a contributor to STEM, education, and community. Depending on the level of sponsorship, Team 79 Krunch offers benefits including:

Brand

Strengthen your image

Sponsoring Krunch reflects your commitment to education, the advancement of STEM, and the well-being of our community.

Network

Build relationships

As both an FIRST® FRC team and a Rotary Interact Club, we connect sponsors with business professionals and civic-minded organizations alike.

Visibility

Boost brand visibility

Your logo can appear on our robot, team t-shirts, and materials at nationally publicized FIRST® competitions and at demonstrations across the Tampa Bay area.

Talent

Build a talent pipeline

Our members have interned and worked for great sponsors including Honeywell, Lockheed Martin, DCM Solutions, Evatech, MITRE, and Southern Manufacturing Technologies.

Engagement

Engage your employees

We offer a broad spectrum of mentor and volunteer opportunities for socially responsible companies and their teams.

Impact

Grow local STEM

Research on FIRST® alumni shows strong STEM outcomes: 83% pursue STEM majors and 63% work in a STEM field. Your gift helps build that pipeline locally.

Ways to Give

More than one way to help

In-Kind Gifts

Donations of goods and services are greatly appreciated. Our immediate and ongoing needs include:

  • Space to build our robot (warehouse, garage, or workshop in North Pinellas / lower Pasco)
  • Robotic parts and tools: drivetrains, motors, control systems, electrical, pneumatics, sensors
  • Personal protection equipment: safety glasses, gloves, ear plugs, first-aid kits
  • Consumables: cleaning supplies, snacks and beverages
  • Marketing: advertising, promotional items, t-shirts, printing
  • A team trailer to transport robots and tools to competitions

Grants, Matching Gifts & Payroll Giving

Over the years, Krunch has been awarded numerous grants. If your organization offers grants aligned with our mission, we would love to apply.

Many employers match employee donations and volunteer hours for educational programs and STEM. Check whether your employer will match your contribution, or set up automatic payroll giving through portals like Benevity or YourCause.
