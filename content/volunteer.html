Mentor & Volunteer

As a FIRST® FRC Competition Team and a Rotary Interact Club, Team 79 Krunch offers a broad spectrum of mentor and volunteer opportunities. Come help inspire and educate our leaders of tomorrow.

Two Ways to Help

Share your skills with the team

Mentors

Become a mentor

Do you have experience in robotics, mechanical, design, electrical, or software engineering and enjoy working with students aged 12 to 18? If so, come help inspire and educate our leaders of tomorrow by becoming a FIRST® Team 79 Krunch mentor.

Volunteers

Volunteer your talents

Are you a business professional with marketing, graphic design, website, or social media expertise? Have you helped other nonprofits with fundraising, grant writing, or community outreach and service projects? Is your hobby photography or videography? If you are looking to have fun while working toward a great cause, we would love to have you join us.

For further details on how you can get involved, or if you have any questions, please reach out to us. We sincerely appreciate and thank you for your consideration and support.
