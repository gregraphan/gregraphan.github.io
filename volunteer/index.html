Mentor & Volunteer

As a FIRST® FRC Competition Team and a Rotary Interact Club, Team 79 Krunch offers a broad spectrum of mentor and volunteer opportunities. Come help inspire and educate our leaders of tomorrow.

Why It Matters

Help inspire the leaders of tomorrow

Mentors and volunteers are the heart of Team 79 Krunch. By sharing a few hours of your time and skills, you help students discover engineering, leadership, and service, and get to watch them grow into confident young professionals. It is one of the most rewarding ways to give back.

How You Can Help

There is a role for every skill set

Many of these need no robotics experience at all, and you can give as much or as little time as your schedule allows.

Mentors

Work directly with students

Share your experience and help students master real skills in:

  • Mechanical design & fabrication
  • Electrical & wiring
  • Programming & controls
  • CAD & engineering design
  • Business, fundraising & project management
  • Safety & shop practices
Volunteers

Lend your talents & time

Help with the many things that keep the team running:

  • Photography & videography
  • Marketing & social media
  • Grant writing & sponsorship
  • Graphic & web design
  • Event chaperones & travel
  • Meals, snacks & logistics on build nights
Good to Know

What to expect

Flexible commitment

We meet Wednesdays at 5:30 pm, and more during build season (January to March), but you give as much or as little time as you can. Even occasional help makes a real difference.

Safe & supported

Team 79 Krunch operates under the FIRST® Youth Protection Program. Lead mentors are background-screened, and volunteers who have not completed screening are never left alone with students.

Come see us first

Not sure where you fit? You are welcome to visit a meeting and see the team in action before you commit. We will help you find the right role.
