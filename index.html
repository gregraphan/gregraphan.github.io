Our Mission

Uniting mentors, engineers, and students through engineering and robotics.

Our goal is to unite mentors, engineers, and students through engineering and robotics. We will learn by using positive attitudes, the latest technology, the will to influence the community, and by committing acts of Gracious Professionalism® on and off the playing field.

We are more than a group of students who meet to build a robot. We are a family that takes care of its community while competing as an award-winning FIRST® Robotics Competition team.

Two Programs, One Team

A robotics team and a service club

Engineering

FIRST® Robotics Competition

FIRST® (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a global nonprofit founded by inventor Dean Kamen. Each season, students design, build, and program a competition robot alongside professional mentors, in an intense engineering sprint.

Community

The Rotary Interact Club

Krunch is also a Rotary Interact Club for youth ages 12–18, part of Rotary International. Members carry out service projects that benefit our community and promote international understanding, while building leadership and citizenship.
