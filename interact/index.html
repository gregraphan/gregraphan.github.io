International + Action

What is Interact?

Team 79 Krunch is also a Rotary Interact Club. Interact gives our students meaningful community service and global connection alongside their engineering.

Launched in 1962, Interact is an international organization of service, part of Rotary International, which fosters leadership and responsible citizenship, and promotes international understanding and peace. The name "Interact" was created by combining the words "international" and "action."

Sponsored by Rotary Clubs, Interact clubs are for youth ages 12–18, and can be associated with a community or a school. Interact club members have fun while carrying out service projects and learning about the world. Every Interact club organizes at least two service projects a year: one that benefits their community and one that encourages international understanding.

While Interact clubs receive guidance from their sponsoring Rotary Clubs, they govern and support themselves. Today, there are over 14,900 Interact clubs with more than 342,000 members in 145 countries. "Interactors" develop skills in leadership and attain practical experience in conducting service projects, thereby learning the satisfaction that comes from serving others.

One of the best things about Interact is that it connects young leaders with Rotary's global community, enabling them to build relationships, learn about other cultures, and exchange ideas with peers worldwide.

The Global Network

Rotary International

Rotary International is a global network of 1.4 million neighbors, friends, leaders, and problem-solvers that see a world where people unite and take action to create lasting change, across the globe, in our communities, and in ourselves.

Solving real problems takes real commitment and vision. For more than 110 years, Rotary's people of action have used their passion, energy, and skills to take action on sustainable projects. Rotary's 46,000+ clubs work together to:

  • Promote peace
  • Fight disease
  • Provide clean water, sanitation, and hygiene
  • Save mothers and children
  • Support education
  • Grow local economies
  • Protect the environment
